Biella, 1825 / History, research and design

The story begins
before the design takes shape.

Founded in Biella in 1825, Serralunga has been run by six generations of the same family. From leatherworking to rotational moulding, the company has put its expertise to work in contemporary planters, furniture and accessories made in Italy.

Each designer brings a fresh perspective, shaping the proportions, details and uses of pieces made to live with every day.
